This temporary website demonstrates a possible digital experience for Car Spa Centre in Ajman.

Independent design preview

Created by PaceSync Systems as a design proposal. Car Spa Centre has not commissioned, approved or endorsed this website. Business names, logo and social images identify the subject of the concept; ownership remains with their respective owners.

Public information and images

Service descriptions, opening hours, location and contacts were taken from the business’s public Facebook, Instagram and Google Maps profiles, reviewed on 29 October 2026. Different public profiles list different contact numbers; both are labelled on the contact page. Prices and appointment availability must be confirmed directly with the business.

Enquiry forms

Form entries stay in your current browser page. There is no form database, account, payment collection or automatic message submission. “Open WhatsApp draft” passes the text you entered to WhatsApp only when you select that link. You decide whether to send it. No appointment is reserved here.

External services

Google Maps is embedded on the contact page. Clicking social, phone, email or WhatsApp links opens the relevant external service, which operates under its own policies. This preview uses no analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies.

Temporary hosting

The preview is intended to be unpublished seven days after deployment unless a different arrangement is agreed. Contact PaceSync Systems at info@pacesyncsystems.com for a correction or removal request.
