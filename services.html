BEFORE YOUR VISIT

A few useful answers.

Which service is right for my car?+

Start with your goal: a fresher interior, a better-looking finish or extra protection. The centre can inspect your vehicle and recommend the right treatment.

Can I book a specific time here?+

You can prepare a preferred date and time on the visit page. This is a concept preview, so no slot is reserved. The team must confirm availability directly.

Are prices and offers confirmed?+

Prices depend on the car and service. Ask the centre for a current quotation, package inclusions and any offer terms before agreeing to work.

Where is the centre?+

Car Spa Centre is listed in Al Jurf / Al Jerf 2, Ajman, UAE. Use the Google Maps directions link for the pinned location.
